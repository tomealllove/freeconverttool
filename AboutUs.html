About Us


Welcome to FreeConvertBox (Chinese Name: 佛博士)!


We are an independent team of passionate internet technology professionals and developers based in Mainland China and Hong Kong. Driven by a shared love for technology, we firmly believe that technical innovation should be leveraged to simplify workflows, boost productivity, and improve everyone's daily lives.


Why We Built This Website


FreeConvertBox.com is a passion project created entirely during our spare time. In our routine work, we noticed that people constantly struggle with tedious tasks like file format conversions, image compression, and PDF management. Unfortunately, many existing online tools either come with expensive price tags or force users to upload sensitive files to their servers, posing significant data privacy risks.To solve this, we built FreeConvertBox. Our platform is defined by being completely free, ultra-efficient, and ready-to-use.
