Privacy Policy / 隐私政策



Last Updated: 2026/09/25

This Privacy Policy describes how FreeConvertBox ("we", "us", or "our") handles data when you use our free online tools at https://freeconvertbox.com. 

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Our website is designed to be used without requiring any user registration or account creation.

1. No Registration & No Personal Data Collection
We do not require users to register an account to use our services. 
- We do not collect personal identification information such as your name, email address, phone number, or billing information. 
- You can use all the file conversion tools on FreeConvertBox completely anonymously.

2. File Processing and Security
To provide our conversion services, your files are ready to use immediately. No uploads, no retention. Your privacy is fully protected.
- No Retention: Neither your original files nor the converted results are retained by us after processing.
- No Access: We do not look at, analyze, or share your files with any third party.

3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising
We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our website to support our free services. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your interests.

- Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- Personalized Advertising: These ads may be tailored based on your browsing history on other websites, but they are not linked to any personal identity on our website.

How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads
You can easily opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting the following official links:
- Google Ads Settings: https://google.com
- Network Advertising Initiative: https://aboutads.info

4. Log Files and Cookies
Like most standard websites, we automatically collect certain non-personal log data through server logs and analytics tools. This includes web browser type, device type, general geographic location (country/city level), referral pages, and timestamps. This data is purely used to analyze trends, administer the site, and improve user experience.

We use standard cookies to remember your preferences (such as language or tool settings). You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options at any time.

5. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
Even though we do not collect or store any personal data that can identify you, we fully respect global privacy laws, including the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). 

Because we do not collect your name, email, or personal details, we have no personal data to disclose, update, or delete upon request. Your anonymity is fully protected.
