FAQ


        
Which ebook formats are supported?

        
EPUB, TXT and HTML input; output as TXT, HTML, EPUB or PDF, covering the most common ebook conversions.

        
Why does the Chinese text come out garbled?

        
That file is not UTF-8 encoded. Tick "Decode as GBK" and convert again so the file is read with GBK.

        
Does EPUB to PDF keep the original layout?

        
Not exactly. Text is reflowed for PDF, and you can set font size, margins and line height. Complex EPUB styling and images are not preserved as intended.

        
Is my ebook uploaded anywhere?

        
No. No uploads, no retention. Your privacy is fully protected.
