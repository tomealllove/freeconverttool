FAQ


        
How is an invoice amount normally written?

        
For example 1234.56 yuan becomes 壹仟贰佰叁拾肆元伍角陆分, with 整 appended for whole amounts. The tool writes it in the standard form automatically.

        
Are there limits on the amount?

        
Integers and two decimal places are supported, which covers day-to-day invoicing, reimbursements and contracts. Very large sums are better split across documents.

        
Can Chinese numerals be converted back to digits?

        
Yes. Use the Chinese numerals to Arabic digits mode, enter 三千五百二十 and get 3520.
