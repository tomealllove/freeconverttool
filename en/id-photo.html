How to make an ID photo

  1. Upload any photo (a front-facing shot with even lighting and a plain background works best).
  2. Choose a size preset such as 1-inch or 2-inch, then pick white, blue, red or a custom background color.
  3. Leave matting on the default "auto · solid color tolerance"; adjust tolerance if the hairline looks rough.
  4. Click Generate, download the single photo, or lay several out onto a 5x6 print sheet.

FAQ

Why did my clothes or hair change color too?

The tolerance is too high. Lower it from the default 60 to around 30-40 and regenerate - especially when wearing light clothing.

Which sizes are supported?

Common presets such as 1-inch and 2-inch are built in, and you can set a custom pixel size. Always confirm the official requirement for your visa or exam application.

Can I print it directly?

Yes. Lay multiple copies out onto a 5x6 sheet, then print at a photo lab or at home and cut them apart.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. No uploads, no retention. Your privacy is fully protected.
