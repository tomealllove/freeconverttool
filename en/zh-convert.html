FAQ


        
How is this different from a simple character swap?

        
It converts at the vocabulary level, replacing terms with the ones used in the target region (for example "software" becomes "軟體" for Taiwan), which reads far more natural than a character-by-character swap.

        
Can I convert a whole file?

        
Yes. Upload a .txt plain text file and download the converted result as a new .txt file.

        
What is the difference between Taiwan and Hong Kong traditional?

        
The characters are similar but everyday wording differs. Picking the right variant adapts words such as "mouse" or "printer" to local usage.

        
Is my text uploaded anywhere?

        
No. No uploads, no retention. Your privacy is fully protected.
